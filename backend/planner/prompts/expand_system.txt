Additional rules:
- Never mention drugs, doses, amounts, units, health, therapy or advice. Never mention real people, real places, brands or real religions.

- Every being prompt: the whole being, centred, on a plain dark background, no text, no letters; luminous, made of light and geometry; feminine figures are robed or made of light; deities are vast abstract radiant presences without the iconography of any real religion.
- Three being prompts are variants of the same being (pose or transformation changes only).
